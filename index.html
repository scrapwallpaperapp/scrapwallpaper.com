Close it, open it
Your home screen

Made for foldables

Closed, your phone shows the cover of your book. Open it, and the book opens: the rest of your photos are inside. Three ways to open: Book, Side by side, Centerpiece. Patent pending.

Regular phones get the cover on the lock screen and a page from inside on the home screen.

Closed, the cover. Open, the book.
Snap, and it's in your book

The Key Photo

Double-tap the Key Photo on your home screen to open Scrap's camera. Your new photo lands in the book with a TAKEN LIVE · OCT 15 stamp, and the full-size photo is saved to your phone's gallery too.

A Key Photo widget does the same with one tap.

Make it yours in the studio

Arrange Polaroids on cork, kraft, lined or plain paper. Tape, pins, photo corners, captions and date stamps. Drag, tilt, resize and layer everything.

Private by design

Scrap keeps your photos on your phone. It has no server, no account, no ads and no tracking, and it removes the hidden location from every photo it saves. Your photos stay yours.

Back your book up any time with "Save a copy". On a new phone, Find my photos puts each photo back in its place.
