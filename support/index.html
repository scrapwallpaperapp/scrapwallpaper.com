Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to scrapwallpaperapp@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

The quickest way from the app: Settings → Send feedback. It fills in your phone's model and its Android or iOS version for you, which helps a lot with problems.

Where are my photos kept?

On your phone, in Scrap's own private storage. Scrap has no server and no account, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Android: photos taken with Scrap's camera are also saved to your phone's gallery, in an album called Scrap.

iPhone: photos taken with Scrap's camera are also saved to your Photos library. Your iPhone's own backup leaves Scrap's photos out, so keep a copy of your book (below).

How do I move my book to a new phone?

In Scrap's Settings, tap "Save a copy of your book" and keep the file somewhere safe: Google Drive on Android, iCloud Drive or Files on iPhone. On the new phone, open Scrap and choose "Open a book file". It's the same file on both, so a book can move from Android to iPhone and back. If your book came back without its pictures, tap "Find my photos" and pick them again: each one goes back in its place.

How do I set my wallpaper on iPhone?

iPhone doesn't let apps change the wallpaper themselves, so Scrap saves your book as pictures and you set them once in Settings. On Scrap's Home, tap "Save to Photos", then "How to set it": Scrap shows the steps for your iPhone. To have your Home Screen show your newest page each time you close Scrap, tap "Make it automatic" and set it up once in the Shortcuts app. It's optional.

Purchases and refunds

Android: Scrap Plus and the packs are bought through Google Play, which also handles refunds. Your purchases come back by themselves on a new phone with the same Google account; if they don't, use "Restore" in Scrap's Settings.

iPhone: Scrap Plus and the packs are bought through the App Store, and Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. On a new iPhone, use the same Apple Account, then tap "Restore" in Scrap's Settings.

Purchases stay with the store they were bought in: Scrap has no account to link them, so something bought on Google Play doesn't carry over to an iPhone, or the other way around.

Privacy

The short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, and your photos stay on your phone. The full privacy policies are here too: Android and iPhone.
